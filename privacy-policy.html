MyAppointment

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

My Appointment ("My Appointment", "we", "our", or "the application") is a smart government appointment booking platform designed to help citizens discover government services, find service centers, view appointment availability, prepare required documents and manage appointments.

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be collected through My Appointment and how that information is used to provide the application's services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used by a citizen, My Appointment may process information necessary to create an account, authenticate the user, manage a profile and support appointment-related functionality.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number, when provided
  • Address, when provided
  • Account authentication information
  • Appointment-related information
  • Required-document selections associated with an appointment

2. Account Information

When a citizen creates an account using email and password, the application processes the information required for account creation and authentication.

Email verification is used for email/password accounts before normal account access is provided.

3. Google Sign-In

My Appointment supports Google Sign-In. When a user chooses Google Sign-In, authentication is performed through Google's authentication service and Firebase Authentication.

The application may receive basic account information made available through the authentication process, such as the user's name and email address, and use that information to create or load the user's My Appointment profile.

4. Profile Information

A user's My Appointment profile may contain information such as name, email address, mobile number and address when those details are provided by the user.

Profile information is used to provide account and application functionality.

5. Appointment Information

When appointment functionality is used, the application may process information associated with the appointment, such as the selected government service, service center, appointment date and time, required-document selections and appointment status.

This information is used to create, display and manage appointments.

6. Location Information

Location functionality may be used when a citizen chooses features that require nearby service-center discovery or directions.

Location access should be requested through the device's permission system. Users can control location permissions through their device settings.

7. Required Documents

My Appointment can provide a checklist of documents associated with a selected government service. Document selections may be associated with an appointment so that the citizen can prepare before visiting a service center.

8. How We Use Information

Information processed by My Appointment may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Maintain citizen profiles.
  • Provide government service information.
  • Support appointment booking and management.
  • Display required-document information.
  • Support service-center discovery.
  • Provide application-related notifications where implemented.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

9. Firebase Services

My Appointment uses Firebase services as part of its application infrastructure. Firebase Authentication is used for user authentication, while Firebase Firestore is used for application data such as user profile information.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable service terms and privacy practices.

10. Information Sharing

My Appointment does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as authentication and cloud infrastructure providers.

11. Data Security

The application uses authentication controls and cloud security mechanisms intended to protect account and application information. Access to application data is controlled through authenticated application workflows and backend security rules.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

12. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant application functionality, maintain account records, support appointments and meet applicable operational requirements.

13. User Choices and Rights

Users may review and update certain profile information through the application. Device permissions, including location permissions, can be controlled through the device operating system.

14. Account and Data Deletion

If you want to request account or personal-data deletion, please contact the My Appointment support team using the contact information provided on the Contact & Support page.

15. Children's Privacy

My Appointment is intended for users who are legally able to use the relevant government appointment services. We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by My Appointment, please visit our Contact & Support page.
