About My Appointment

A citizen-focused approach to government appointment management.

My Appointment provides a centralized digital experience for government-related appointment services. Citizens can discover available services, identify suitable service centers, view available appointment slots, prepare required documents and manage their appointments from one platform.


The platform is designed around the Smart Government Appointment System requirements of DT-16, with an emphasis on reducing unnecessary visits, improving appointment organization and helping citizens prepare before visiting a service center.

Security & Privacy

My Appointment is designed with secure account and data handling in mind.

Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used for account authentication. Email verification is supported for email/password accounts.

Cloud Data

User profile information is stored using Firebase Firestore as part of the application's backend.
